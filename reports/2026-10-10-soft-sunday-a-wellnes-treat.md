# Soft Sunday - Yin Yoga ✮ Sound Healing ✮ Indian Dinner — landing page audit
URL: https://almawellness.io/experiencias/soft-sunday-a-wellnes-treat
Audited: 2026-10-10

## Summary
Structurally this page is fine — clear single CTA, clear pricing, strong multi-host trust signals, full logistics. But the actual copy has the most brand-voice violations seen in an audit so far: it's written almost entirely in generic, salesy English wellness-marketing prose (not Alma's Spanish/tú-voice default), with ALL-CAPS subheadings throughout, two exclamation marks, and decorative emoji/star glyphs — including in the title itself. This reads like unedited host-submitted copy rather than something curated into Alma's voice.

## Brand voice findings
- **ALL CAPS subheadings used for emphasis, repeatedly:** "THE EVENING," "THE EXPERIENCE," "YIN YOGA," "SOUND HEALING," "INDIAN DINNER," "INTENTIONAL CONNECTION," "WHAT YOU'LL LEAVE WITH," "MEET YOUR HOSTS." This isn't an isolated slip — it's the page's entire heading system, and it directly violates the sentence-case rule.
- **Exclamation marks in visible copy** (hard rule: none, anywhere):
  - Arati's quote: "...our stories, music and even our clothes! That's why I want to welcome you all..."
  - Javi's bio tagline: "Culinary Host · Foodie · **Your new best friend!**"
- **Decorative emoji/glyphs in copy, including the title itself:**
  - Page title: "Soft Sunday - Yin Yoga **✮** Sound Healing **✮** Indian Dinner" — star glyphs used as separators in the single most visible piece of copy on the page.
  - "**✨** SOFT SUNDAY - A Sunday evening to slow down, reconnect & replenish."
  - "**🤍** INTENTIONAL CONNECTION" and the closing line "Come to receive and leave replenished **🤍**."
  - The shared "Lo que te llevas" icon row (🌿✦🌬🤍) also appears here, same as on the cacao listing — confirms this is a template-level issue, not one-off.
- **Generic/salesy tone instead of Alma's warm-contemplative register:** "an intimate sanctuary designed for the givers, the creators, and the guides who constantly pour into others" and "give your nervous system room to exhale, share space with a mindful community, and leave completely replenished" read like stock wellness-brand ad copy — long, breezy, aspirational — not the short, deliberate, "every word chosen" tone the brand book calls for. Compare this to the cacao listing's much more measured Spanish paragraph.
- **Edges toward overclaimed therapeutic benefit:** under "YIN YOGA," bullets state the practice "can help you... Release accumulated muscular tension" and "Counter some of the physical effects of prolonged sitting and screen time." This reads as a therapeutic-outcomes list rather than a curatorial description of what the session offers — not as severe as a medical "cures" claim, but worth tightening so Alma isn't implicitly promising treatment outcomes.
- **Voice inconsistency:** the UI chrome (Itinerario, Lo que te llevas, Qué incluye, Política de cancelación) is in Spanish, but the entire long-form description is in English — unlike every other audited listing. This feels like host copy dropped in unedited rather than adapted into Alma's voice; worth a deliberate editorial pass either way.

## Conversion UX findings
- **Primary CTA:** clear and singular — "Reservar mi lugar →," with a sticky CTA bar. Easy to find.
- **Trust signals:** strong — four separate, detailed host bios (Frida, Kyla, Arati, Javi) with real credentials and backstory. No reviews/testimonials and no on-page "verified" badge (same gap as the cacao listing). Cancellation policy is clearly tiered (100%/50%/0%).
- **Pricing:** clear and singular — $1,880 MXN/person, plus an optional +$120 MXN flexible-cancellation add-on. No hidden costs.
- **Logistics upfront:** date (Sun Oct 11, 2026 — notably just one day out from this audit, with only "5 lugares" left), check-in time, 4h duration, group cap (12), bilingual (Español, Inglés), full address with map link, what's included, what to bring.
- **Friction:** the "THE EXPERIENCE" section is a long wall of English sub-essays (Yin Yoga / Sound Healing / Indian Dinner, each several paragraphs) — readable because of subheads, but noticeably denser than other listings. All images carry `alt=""`, same gap as elsewhere. Mobile viewport meta tag is present.

## Recommended changes, prioritized
1. **Critical** — Convert all ALL-CAPS subheadings to sentence case, and remove the two exclamation marks (Arati's quote, Javi's tagline). These are the most visible, most repeated brand-rule breaks found in any audit so far.
2. **Critical** — Strip the decorative ✮ glyphs from the page title and the ✨/🤍 emoji from the body copy, plus the shared 🌿✦🌬🤍 icon row (same fix needed on the cacao listing — fix once at the template level).
3. **Important** — Edit the English long-form sections into Alma's shorter, more contemplative voice; cut the generic "sanctuary for the givers..." marketing language and soften the yoga-benefits bullets so they read as curatorial description, not a therapeutic-outcomes list.
4. **Important** — Add descriptive alt text to the experience images.
5. **Nice to have** — Decide and document whether host-submitted copy is allowed to stay in its original language (English here) or should always be localized/edited into Alma's Spanish tú-voice — right now it looks like an inconsistency rather than a deliberate choice.
