# TTRE y Tambor Chamánico — landing page audit
URL: https://almawellness.io/experiencias/ttre-y-tambor-chamanico
Audited: 2026-11-14

## Summary
This page has real structural problems, not just polish issues: it uses emoji throughout (a hard "never" in the brand book) and makes several overclaimed therapeutic/medical statements about trauma release and neuroendocrine/brainwave effects that Alma's brand rules explicitly forbid. On top of the voice issues, the booking widget itself looks broken — selecting more spots doesn't update the total, and the advertised duration doesn't match the listed itinerary. This is the most brand-rule-violating new listing seen in recent runs and should go to a human quickly.

## Brand voice findings
- **Emoji, hard violation (zero tolerance):** 📍 next to the location, 💎 on three benefit bullets, 🔥 before "Te espero en un espacio neutral", and 🌿 ✦ 🌬 🤍 on the four takeaway labels ("Transformación", "Presencia", "Calma", "Conexión"). The brand book says no emoji, anywhere — this page uses at least seven.
- **Overclaimed therapeutic/medical results, hard violation:** copy describes TTRE as "un mecanismo orgánico del cuerpo para la liberación de trauma y estrés crónico" and claims "la modulación de parámetros neuroendocrinos vinculados al estrés" and the ability to "sincronizar las ondas cerebrales —promoviendo, por ejemplo, ondas teta—". These are specific physiological/clinical claims (trauma release, neuroendocrine modulation, brainwave entrainment) stated as fact with no caveats or sourcing — exactly the "esto cura tu ansiedad"-style overclaim the brand book prohibits. Alma curates experiences; it shouldn't read like it's promising clinical outcomes.
- **No exclamation marks** — compliant.
- **No empty superlatives or fake urgency** — "2 lugares por $1,200 · ahorran $100 cada uno" is a plain bundle discount, not discount-shouting, and reads fine.
- **Tone drifts into jargon rather than contemplativo:** "modulación de parámetros neuroendocrinos" and "regulación bioenergética y sanación natural" read as dense/clinical-sounding marketing copy, not the short, warm, deliberate sentences the brand book asks for. The host's personal framing ("Te invito a probar una sesión de liberación profunda", "No requieres experiencia previa, solamente ganas de liberarte y estar en equilibrio") is in the right register and should be the model for trimming the rest.
- Casing on labels (e.g. "Qué incluye", "No incluye") is sentence case and fine; no ALL CAPS or Title Case emphasis found in visible copy besides the stylized H1 "_CHAMÁNICO_", which reads as italic emphasis rather than caps-for-shouting.

## Conversion UX findings
- **Pricing widget bug:** the quantity selector allows up to "4 lugares" but the Total still shows $700 (the single-person price) regardless of quantity selected. This is a functional bug, not a copy issue — it will either undercharge multi-person bookings or confuse buyers at checkout.
- **Duration mismatch:** the listing advertises a 2-hour session, but the published itinerary runs 12:00–13:40, i.e. 1h40. Minor but erodes trust in the listed logistics.
- **Primary CTA is clear and persistent:** "Reservar" / "Reservar mi lugar →" appears multiple times including a sticky footer — easy to find without excessive scrolling.
- **Trust signals are thin:** no reviews, ratings, or testimonials anywhere on the page, and no verification badge on the host despite a fairly elaborate credentials list (reiki master, musicoterapia certification, "6 certificaciones en Gong," a cited government wellness program). The claims would land better with even one review or a verification mark.
- **Cancellation policy is clear and tiered** (100% / 50% / credit-only / no-refund bands by days-before, plus an optional flexible-cancellation add-on) — this is a good trust signal and matches the brand book's "honesto" value.
- **Pricing is visible but not fully clear:** base price, companion rate, and pair bundle are all shown, but combined with the quantity-selector bug above, a buyer can't actually tell what they'll be charged for 2+ people.
- **Logistics are mostly complete:** date, check-in time, exact address with a Maps link, group size cap (9 personas), language, what's included/not included, and what to bring are all present.

## Recommended changes, prioritized
1. **Critical** — Remove all emoji from the page (location pin, benefit bullets, takeaway icons). This is a non-negotiable brand rule.
2. **Critical** — Rewrite the trauma/neuroendocrine/brainwave claims to describe the practice and the host's intent rather than asserting specific physiological outcomes; add Alma's standard disclaimer language distinguishing curated wellness experiences from medical/therapeutic treatment.
3. **Critical** — Fix the quantity-selector total so it scales with "lugares" selected; this is a checkout-trust issue, not cosmetic.
4. **Important** — Correct either the stated 2h duration or the itinerary end time so they agree.
5. **Important** — Add at least one trust signal (a review, a verification badge, or a link substantiating the "Cartelera Bienestar del Gobierno de Morelos" claim) given how much the page leans on the host's credentials to justify the price.
6. **Nice to have** — Simplify the clinical-sounding phrasing ("modulación de parámetros neuroendocrinos") into warmer, shorter language consistent with the rest of the host's voice.
