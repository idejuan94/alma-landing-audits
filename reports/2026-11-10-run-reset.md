# Run & Reset — landing page audit
URL: https://almawellness.io/experiencias/run-reset
Audited: 2026-11-10

## Summary
This page has structural problems, not just polish issues: it leans on e-commerce discount framing and a scattershot English/Spanish lifestyle-ad tone that doesn't match Alma's contemplative voice, it's dense with emoji, and the logistics section has enough internal contradictions (mismatched check-in times, an undefined second group, two different names for the instructor) that a reader can't actually tell what they're booking.

## Brand voice findings
- **ALL CAPS headline** — H1 reads "RUN & _RESET_", title-cased/all-caps for emphasis rather than sentence case.
- **Heavy emoji use** — 🌿 🌬 🤍 📍 🕐 🎟 appear across the page; this is Alma's most emoji-dense page audited so far and clearly violates the no-emoji rule.
- **Discount-shouting / e-commerce pricing language** — a struck-through "$1,150 MXN" next to "$950 MXN," explicitly labeled "Preventa" and "early bird, −17%." This is exactly the false-urgency/e-commerce framing the brand book prohibits, even without an exclamation mark attached.
- **Tone reads as generic lifestyle-brand marketing, not "cálido, contemplativo"** — "Tu nuevo Sunday plan: 5K con Connie, sauna & cold plunge, un self tanning moment express con Sunkissed..." mixes English marketing phrases ("self tanning moment," "cold plunge," "crew") into Spanish copy in a way that reads like an Instagram ad for a lifestyle pop-up, not a curated wellness experience description. Alma's voice calls for short sentences with room to breathe — this is a breathless feature-list sentence.
- No exclamation marks and no medical overclaiming were found, which is correct.

## Conversion UX findings
- **Contradictory logistics** — the itinerary states check-in at 7:45 a.m. and 8:00 a.m., but the booking selector only offers 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 a.m. slots. There's no itinerary provided for the 9:00 a.m. group at all, and the body mentions "Grupo 1: 8:00–10:45" with no "Grupo 2" ever defined. A buyer booking the 9 a.m. slot has no way to know what their morning actually looks like.
- **Instructor named two different ways** — "Connie" in the headline copy vs. "Constanza Ramos" elsewhere on the page. Minor on its own, but combined with the schedule contradictions it makes the whole page feel unreviewed before publishing.
- **Mentioned but unlisted brand** — "Miceila Labs" is named in the body copy but doesn't appear in the included-items or brand-partner list, leaving its role in the experience unclear.
- Trust signals are missing: no reviews, testimonials, instructor credentials, or verification badge — only a brand description block ("Sobre MindEase experiences").
- What's working: pricing (even setting aside the discount framing) is clearly stated, cancellation tiers are detailed and specific (100% / 50%-or-credit / credit-only / none, by days-out), there's a single primary CTA ("Reservar mi lugar →," sticky footer repeats it), and the venue has a Google Maps link.
- Minor: "Inspirarme" and "Experiencias" in the nav both link to `/experiencias` — a redundant destination worth fixing but low priority.

## Recommended changes, prioritized
1. **Critical** — Resolve the schedule contradictions: align check-in times between the itinerary and the booking selector, add an itinerary for the 9:00 a.m. group, and either define "Grupo 2" or remove references to it.
2. **Critical** — Remove the struck-through "was/now" discount price and "Preventa"/"−17%" labeling; if early pricing is legitimate, state it plainly without e-commerce urgency framing. Fix the all-caps headline and remove all emoji.
3. **Important** — Rewrite the intro copy in Alma's voice — shorter sentences, less feature-listing, no mixed-language marketing phrases — and pick one name for the instructor and use it everywhere.
4. **Nice to have** — Clarify what "Miceila Labs" contributes to the experience, fix the redundant "Inspirarme"/"Experiencias" nav link, and add instructor credentials or any trust signal beyond the brand-story paragraph.
