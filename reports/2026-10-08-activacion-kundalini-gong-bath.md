# Activación kundalini+ Gong Bath — landing page audit
URL: https://almawellness.io/experiencias/activacion-kundalini-gong-bath
Audited: 2026-10-08

## Summary
Structurally this page is solid — logistics, pricing, cancellation terms, and a single clear CTA are all present and easy to find. But it's riddled with emoji, which is a hard brand-voice violation and pulls the tone toward generic Instagram-caption wellness marketing rather than Alma's curated, contemplative voice. The emoji usage is the one issue that needs a real fix; everything else is minor polish.

## Brand voice findings
- **Emoji, repeatedly, in violation of the hard "no emoji, anywhere" rule.** The headline itself is `Activación kundalini+ Gong Bath✨`. The intro tagline ends with "...despertar tu energía vital. ✨". The closing line of the main description reads "Una pausa para tu mente, un abrazo para tu cuerpo y un espacio para tu alma. 🤍" — genuinely well-written, contemplative copy, undercut by the emoji tacked on. The itinerary section header is "✨ Itinerario | Kundalini Activation + Gong Bath", individual itinerary rows are "Kundalini Activation ✨" and "Closing Sound Bath 🤍", the location line is "📍 The Somatic Room", the date line is "🗓 Sábado 10 de octubre | 1:00 PM", and the four takeaway tags each carry their own emoji (🌿 Transformación, ✦ Presencia, 🌬 Calma, 🤍 Conexión). The host-bio paragraph also closes on "...todo lo que hemos aprendido a cargar. 🤍". This is the single clearest, most repeated brand violation on the page.
- **Title Case used for English section labels, inconsistent with the otherwise sentence-case Spanish copy.** The itinerary rows are labeled "Opening Sound Bath", "Kundalini Activation ✨", and "Closing Sound Bath 🤍" — Title Case in English, sitting next to sentence-case Spanish headings like "Qué incluye" and "Sobre The Somatic Room by Fernanda Quiñónez". It reads like a copy-paste from a yoga-studio schedule rather than deliberate, curated copy.
- **No usted, no exclamation marks, no empty superlatives or false urgency, no overclaimed medical results** — all confirmed clean. Copy consistently uses tú ("te recostarás", "te dejarás envolver", "te invitamos a soltar"), there isn't a single "!" anywhere on the page, and there's no "la mejor experiencia" / "#1" language or countdown-style urgency. The host's credentials ("psicopedagoga, terapeuta Cognitivo Conductual") are stated as her background, not as a promised cure.
- **Tone is otherwise genuinely on-brand.** Lines like "En un ambiente íntimo, seguro y amoroso, te invitamos a soltar el control, dejar de hacer y simplemente recibir" and the host bio ("Hay emociones que necesitan sentirse, historias que el cuerpo guarda...") read as warm and contemplative, with short paragraphs and room to breathe — exactly the voice Alma wants. The emoji are really the only thing fighting against it.

## Conversion UX findings
- **Single, clear primary CTA, visible without scrolling.** "Reservar mi lugar →" appears in a sticky top bar alongside the price (visible on load) and again at the bottom of the page as "Reservar →". No competing or ambiguous CTAs.
- **Logistics are fully upfront**: date and time (Sábado 10 de octubre, 1:00 PM), duration (2h), group size (Grupo de 10 personas), languages (Inglés, Español), and address with a "Abrir ubicación en Google Maps →" link. "Qué incluye" lists what's covered (Sound bath con Gong).
- **Pricing is clear and singular**: $1,300 MXN per person, with one clearly optional add-on (+$120 MXN for flexible cancellation) — no hidden tiers or surprise costs.
- **Cancellation policy is mostly clear but has a gap**: it states "+14 días: reembolso completo (100%)" and "7 a 14 días: reembolso 50% o crédito completo (50%)", but says nothing about what happens if you cancel fewer than 7 days out. Worth stating explicitly (even if it's 0%) rather than leaving it implied.
- **No reviews, ratings, or host-verification badge anywhere on the page.** The only trust signals are the host bio and the "Sin cargo hasta confirmar · Pago seguro" line. For a marketplace positioning itself as curated, the complete absence of any social proof or verification marker on an experience listing is a gap worth addressing platform-wide, not just on this page.
- **Every image on the page has an empty `alt=""` attribute** (all 6 `<img>` tags, covering the 3 unique background/gallery images). This is a real accessibility and SEO miss, not a one-off oversight.
- **Mobile viewport meta tag is present and correct** (`width=device-width, initial-scale=1`) — no issue there.
- **No walls of text, no obviously broken links, no egregious page weight** — the HTML document itself is ~39KB and images are served from Supabase storage without any visible red flags in the markup.

## Recommended changes, prioritized
1. **Critical** — Strip every emoji from the page (headline, tagline, body copy close, itinerary headers/rows, location/date lines, takeaway tags, host bio close). This is a hard, non-negotiable brand rule and it's violated in at least 10 distinct places on this single listing.
2. **Important** — Add real `alt` text to all experience images (currently all empty). Add a stated cancellation outcome for the 0–7-day window instead of leaving it unaddressed. Consider adding at least one reviews/verification element, even if minimal, given the marketplace's curated positioning.
3. **Nice to have** — Make the itinerary row labels sentence case and consistent with the rest of the page's Spanish copy instead of Title Case English ("Opening Sound Bath" → something like "Baño de sonido inicial", or at minimum drop the Title Case).
