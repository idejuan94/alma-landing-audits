# Alaya 14 - Anima — landing page audit
URL: https://almawellness.io/experiencias/alaya-14-anima
Audited: 2026-10-27

## Summary
The page is conceptually strong — a well-written, contemplative ceremony description with clear logistics and a fair cancellation policy — but it breaks two hard brand rules outright (ALL CAPS headline/details block and heavy emoji use) and is thin on trust signals. The conversion mechanics (single CTA, pricing, logistics) are solid.

## Brand voice findings
- **ALL CAPS, hard violation:** The hero headline renders as `☄️ ALAYA 14 · ANIMA LO QUE MUERE Y LO QUE PERMANECE` and the subhead as `HONRAR · SOLTAR · RENACER`, both in all caps. The "DETALLES DE LA EXPERIENCIA" section label is also caps. Brand book requires sentence case everywhere, no exception for emphasis — this needs to be re-cased to something like "Alaya 14 · Anima lo que muere y lo que permanece."
- **Emoji, hard violation:** 58 emoji characters appear across the page, e.g. `🌙 Ana 🐚Cristina Ficachi · TARA`, `📅 Domingo 1 de noviembre de 2026`, `🕕 6:06 PM a 8:08 PM`, `📍 Colonia Condesa, Ciudad de México`, `🖤 Código de vestimenta: negro`, `👥 Cupo especial: 21 almas`. Alma's rules are "no emoji, anywhere" — these are used as bullet-point icons throughout the details block and host-credit line, not just decoratively, so this is a structural pattern, not a one-off slip.
- **No exclamation marks found** — checked the raw markup; the only `!` characters present are inside HTML comments (`<!--`), not copy. Compliant.
- **tú/usted: compliant.** No "usted" forms detected. The CTA reads "Reservar mi lugar →" — a first-person claim, which the brand book explicitly allows.
- **No empty superlatives or false urgency.** "Cupo especial: 21 almas" is a real, stated capacity limit tied to the ceremony's symbolic "21 almas" concept, not manufactured scarcity or countdown pressure — reads as honest, not salesy.
- **No overclaimed therapeutic/medical language.** Copy uses emotional/ritual language ("recuperar nuestra capacidad de elegir," "despedirse de lo que ya no te permite vivirla") but stops short of clinical claims like curing anxiety or replacing therapy.
- **Tone is otherwise a strong match for cálido/contemplativo/curado.** The closing line "Honra lo que fue, elige lo que viene…" and the framing around three tarot arcana (Muerte, Templanza, Mundo) read as deliberate and un-corporate — this is good, worth preserving once the caps/emoji issues are fixed.

## Conversion UX findings
- **Single clear CTA, good placement:** "Reservar mi lugar" appears as a sticky/mobile price bar plus inline button — easy to find without excessive scrolling.
- **Pricing is clear:** $1,021 MXN per person, with an optional flexible-cancellation add-on (+$120 MXN) stated plainly, and a quantity selector (1–21) with dynamic total. No hidden-cost impression.
- **Cancellation policy is explicit and reasonable:** 100% refund 15+ days out, 50% at 8–14 days, 0% inside 7 days, plus a paid flexible option — this is a genuine trust signal and should be highlighted as a model for other listings.
- **Logistics are upfront:** date/time (Sun Nov 1, 2026, 6:06–8:08 PM), exact address near Parque México with a Maps link, group size (21), language, dress code, and included/excluded items (ceremony/ritual/sound included; transport not included) are all present before booking.
- **Trust signals are thin:** no reviews, ratings, past-attendee count, or verification badge. The only credibility cue is the host's self-reported "+5 años" experience line — no credentials, certifications, or third-party proof. Worth adding at least a short host credential or a link to past-event photos/testimonials.
- **Minor technical note:** mobile viewport meta tag is present and correctly configured (`width=device-width, initial-scale=1`), so no mobile-responsiveness concern. Image `alt` attributes could not be confirmed as populated (rendered markup showed empty `alt=""` patterns in the fetched render) — worth a manual check for accessibility, though not severe enough to call it broken.

## Recommended changes, prioritized
1. **Critical** — Remove all-caps styling from the hero headline, subhead, and "DETALLES DE LA EXPERIENCIA" label; convert to sentence case per brand book.
2. **Critical** — Strip the emoji icons from the details block and host-credit line (dates, location, dress code, host names). Replace with plain text or a non-emoji icon system if visual bullets are wanted.
3. **Important** — Add a real trust signal: host certification/training mention, a link to past ceremony photos, or attendee testimonials — the host bio is currently just a self-reported years-of-experience claim with nothing to back it.
4. **Nice to have** — Verify `alt` text is populated on listing images for accessibility.
