# UNISON: Existir es un arte — landing page audit
URL: https://almawellness.io/experiencias/unison
Audited: 2026-11-26

## Summary
Structurally this page is in good shape — clear CTA, transparent pricing, a detailed cancellation policy, and genuinely intimate second-person copy — but it shares the same template-level emoji violation found on the other new listing audited today, and the host bio makes an unsubstantiated wellness/identity claim ("niño índigo") plus a modality claim (ThetaHealing) with no caveat about Alma curating rather than providing therapeutic treatment. There's also a real information gap: the page advertises a 13-person group but the booking selector caps at 8.

## Brand voice findings
- **Real emoji in the outcomes row, confirmed in source:** identical pattern to the `breath-in-sound-sigie-tus-suenos` listing — `<span class="outcome-emoji">🌿</span>` (Transformación), `✦` (Presencia), `🌬` (Calma), `🤍` (Conexión), plus a `📍` pin on the location line. This is a hard violation of the "no emoji, anywhere" rule and, appearing on two separate listings the same day, looks like a shared component rather than a one-off copy mistake.
- **Unsubstantiated identity/wellness claim without a disclaimer:** the host bio reads "crecí identificándome como un niño índigo" and credits a personal transformation to "ThetaHealing" — ("vi de cerca el cambio que vivió mi papá con ThetaHealing"). Neither claim is medical, but both lean into unverified wellness-identity language that sits close to the "no overclaimed therapeutic/medical results" line, especially combined with the next point.
- **Sensitive claim with no "this isn't a substitute for professional care" framing:** "Mi intención es llevar este trabajo a personas que transitan una enfermedad terminal y ofrecerles, a través del sonido, un espacio de pausa y compañía en esa etapa." Offering companionship to people with a terminal illness is a meaningful, sincere statement, but on a brand that explicitly curates rather than replaces professional treatment, this reads as the kind of claim that should carry (or at least imply) that caveat rather than standing alone as a therapeutic promise.
- **Good example of the intended tone:** "Para cerrar, te invito a recostarte mientras toco los cuencos de cuarzo." — short, sensory, second person, no hard-sell language. The rest of the itinerary description reads the same way.
- **No exclamation marks, no Title Case, no e-commerce urgency language** — "UNISON" itself is styled in caps but reads as a brand/logo treatment rather than emphasis-via-caps in body copy, so it's a softer call than the other listing's mid-sentence "TUS."

## Conversion UX findings
- **Single clear CTA**, reinforced the same way as other listings: "Reservar mi lugar →" in the form plus a sticky "Reservar →" footer button.
- **Pricing is transparent:** $850 MXN per person, with the same optional +$120 MXN flexible-cancellation add-on itemized rather than hidden.
- **Cancellation terms are clear:** 100% refund >15 days, 50% at 8–14 days, no refund ≤7 days — though note this policy is *more* restrictive than the breath-work listing's tiers (no partial-credit middle tier), so it's worth confirming that's intentional per-host variation and not a copy error.
- **Group-size inconsistency:** the page states "Grupo de 13 personas" in the logistics block, but the "Cuántos lugares" quantity selector only offers options 1–8. If 13 is the total session capacity and 8 is just a per-booking cap, that's reasonable but currently unexplained; as written it reads as a mismatch a prospective guest could notice and find confusing.
- **"No incluye" framed with a red × marker:** sauna and cold plunge are listed under "No incluye" with a red visual marker, but it's not stated whether those amenities even exist at the venue (OWA Roma Norte) — worth clarifying so it doesn't read as "here's something you're missing out on."
- **Missing alt text on all six experience images**, confirmed in the raw HTML (`alt=""` throughout).
- **No reviews, ratings, or social proof** anywhere on the page — the only credibility signal is the host's personal bio, which (per above) leans on unverified identity/modality claims rather than formal credentials.

## Recommended changes, prioritized
1. **Critical** — Remove the emoji from the outcomes row (🌿 ✦ 🌬 🤍) and the 📍 pin; same fix as the other new listing today, likely fixable once at the shared template level.
2. **Important** — Add a line (even a short, standard footer disclaimer across all listings) clarifying that Alma curates these experiences and they don't replace professional medical or mental-health care, given this bio explicitly references supporting people with terminal illness.
3. **Important** — Resolve or explain the "13 personas" vs. 8-max-per-booking discrepancy so it doesn't look like a bug to a prospective guest.
4. **Nice to have** — Add alt text to the six experience images, and clarify whether sauna/cold plunge actually exist on-site before listing them as explicit exclusions.
