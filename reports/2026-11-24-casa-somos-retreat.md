# Casa Somos Retreat — landing page audit
URL: https://almawellness.io/experiencias/casa-somos-retreat
Audited: 2026-11-24

## Summary
Structurally solid — clear single CTA, transparent pricing, a real cancellation policy, and consistent tú voice with sentence-case headings and no exclamation marks. The page is let down by one hard brand-voice violation (emoji use throughout) and a dense, unbroken "About Casa Somos" paragraph that hurts readability.

## Brand voice findings
- **Emoji present — hard rule violation.** The page uses 📍, 🌿, 🌬, 🤍 (twice), 🪽 as inline icons/decoration. Alma's brand book says "no emoji, anywhere," with no exception for icon-style use.
- **Tú used correctly throughout.** Copy like "Lo que te llevas," "Elige una fecha," and "¿Quieres cancelación flexible…?" stays second-person informal — no usted slips.
- **No exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS, no Title Case headlines.** Headings ("Qué incluye," "No incluye," "Qué llevar") are sentence case throughout.
- **No empty superlatives or false urgency found.** "Grupo de 8 personas" is a factual capacity line, not manufactured scarcity.
- **Tone mostly lands as intended (cálido, curado).** The host note — "Soy Pao, creadora de Casa Somos" — reads personal rather than corporate. The phrase "el poder de crecer juntas" leans slightly toward generic retreat-marketing language but isn't an overclaimed therapeutic promise.

## Conversion UX findings
- **Single clear primary CTA, well-placed.** "Reservar mi lugar →" on the booking card plus a sticky "Reservar →" bottom bar means the CTA is visible without much scrolling.
- **Pricing is fully transparent.** $24,444 MXN per person, quantity selector (1–4), explicit inclusion/exclusion lists, and an optional +$120 MXN flexible-cancellation add-on. No hidden costs implied.
- **Logistics are upfront:** dates (18–21 Mar, year given only in body copy as "March 2027" — worth pulling the year into the date widget itself), check-in time, group size (8), languages, and a Google Maps link.
- **Trust signals are present but thin.** A personal host bio exists, but there are no stated credentials/certifications, no reviews or social proof, and no verification badge. The cancellation policy itself is a solid trust signal (tiered refund table, 100% at 14+ days down to no refund under 48h).
- **Readability friction: "About Casa Somos" is one unbroken block of text** covering origin story, past workshops, the 2027 retreat, and practices offered all in a single paragraph — it should be broken into shorter paragraphs or subheadings.
- **Minor copy duplication:** the itinerary labels repeat ("Día 1: Día 1 — Conectar"), and "Inspirarme" in the nav links to the same destination as "Experiencias," which is redundant.
- **Possible image duplication:** each image tag appears to repeat the same URL — may be an intentional carousel, but worth a human spot-check that real distinct photos are loading.

## Recommended changes, prioritized
1. **Critical** — Remove all emoji (📍, 🌿, 🌬, 🤍, 🪽) from the page; replace with plain text labels or non-emoji iconography.
2. **Important** — Break the "About Casa Somos" paragraph into shorter paragraphs or add subheadings; it's the densest, least scannable part of the page.
3. **Important** — Add host credentials/qualifications and any available reviews or testimonials to strengthen trust signals beyond the personal bio.
4. **Nice to have** — Show the retreat year (2027) directly next to the date range in the booking widget, not just in body copy; fix the duplicated "Día 1" label; deduplicate the "Inspirarme"/"Experiencias" nav link; spot-check that the image carousel isn't serving the same photo twice.
