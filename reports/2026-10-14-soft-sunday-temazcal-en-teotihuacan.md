# Soft Sunday - Temazcal en Teotihuacán — landing page audit
URL: https://almawellness.io/experiencias/soft-sunday-temazcal-en-teotihuacan
Audited: 2026-10-14

## Summary
This page has the most serious brand-voice problem seen in recent audits: unqualified healing/curative language around a temazcal ceremony, with no safety disclaimer, sitting right next to emoji and ALL CAPS. Conversion UX is otherwise reasonable (clear pricing, full itinerary, cancellation terms) but the booking panel sits unusually low on the page and the English/Spanish copy is fully duplicated rather than curated per-language.

## Brand voice findings
- "healing," "sanación," "curandera," and "traditional healing practices" appear describing the temazcal and the host's background, with no disclaimer about contraindications (heat, steam, herbs — pregnancy, heart conditions, medication interactions, etc. are never mentioned). Alma's brand rule is explicit: curate the selection, don't imply medical/therapeutic results. This reads as an overclaimed-outcome violation, not just borderline tone.
- "Join us for a special SOFT SUNDAY in Teotihuacán" / "Acompáñanos a un SOFT SUNDAY especial en Teotihuacán" — ALL CAPS used for emphasis on the event name in running copy. Should be sentence case; bold/italic if emphasis is needed.
- "Lo que te llevas" icons (🌿 ✦ 🌬 🤍) plus a 📍 marker — same emoji pattern flagged on other listings. No exclamation marks found — compliant there.
- Superlatives are soft but frequent ("special," "beautiful," "nourishing," "playful," "grounding") — none are empty-superlative violations on their own ("la mejor..." etc. not present), but stacked together across two full bilingual descriptions they read closer to generic wellness-marketing copy than the "curado" (every word deliberate) standard — e.g. "A chance to step away from the pace of everyday life, return to your body, share a beautiful meal... and spend a Sunday simply being" is pleasant but could be said in a third of the words.
- Decorative text separators ("─── ⋆⋅☆⋅⋆ ───") between the English and Spanish blocks are a stylistic tic that reads as templated rather than curated.

## Conversion UX findings
- Single CTA ("Reservar mi lugar →") exists but the main booking panel appears only after the gallery, both language descriptions, the full itinerary, inclusions/packing list, host bio, and cancellation policy — a visitor has to scroll past a lot before the primary booking panel. The sticky bottom bar mitigates this, but the first above-the-fold CTA placement should be checked.
- Host bio (Frida Ayala) claims certification in "Yoga, Ayurveda, and Women's Health" but names no issuing body, and describes temazcal training as two years learning from a shaman plus a curandera great-grandmother — for a heat/ritual ceremony this is exactly the kind of claim that needs either more specificity or should be toned down, not both vague and health-adjacent.
- No reviews, ratings, or attendee testimonials. Only soft scarcity ("14 lugares") and payment reassurance ("Sin cargo hasta confirmar · Pago seguro").
- Pricing is a single clear tier ($1,499 MXN/person) plus the same named cancellation add-on seen elsewhere (+$120 MXN) — no hidden costs, but the plain "$1,499" header omits the currency while the panel and sticky bar show "MXN."
- What's included is listed (temazcal, vocal activation, lunch, transport) but what's NOT included is never stated — visitors are left guessing whether anything (e.g. the towel-swap item, drinks) costs extra.
- Cancellation tiers have a gap: "more than 15 days" gets 100%, "8–14 days" gets 50% — the 15-day boundary itself isn't assigned to either tier.
- Full English/Spanish duplication (description, itinerary, packing list) roughly doubles the reading load and reads as pasted-in rather than curated per language.
- All images have empty alt text — accessibility gap.
- Location link is a generic "Teotihuacán, MX" map search rather than the actual meeting point/venue.

## Recommended changes, prioritized
1. **Critical** — Remove or substantially soften the healing/curandera/"traditional healing practices" language, or add a clear disclaimer that Alma curates experiences and does not provide medical or therapeutic treatment. Also fix the ALL CAPS "SOFT SUNDAY" instances and remove all emoji — these are non-negotiable brand rules, and the health-claim one carries real liability risk given it involves heat/steam ceremony.
2. **Important** — State what's NOT included, fix the 15-day cancellation boundary gap, add a specific venue/meeting-point link instead of a generic city search, and give the host's certifications a named issuing body (or drop the specific claim if it can't be substantiated).
3. **Nice to have** — Trim the bilingual copy instead of fully duplicating it, add alt text to images, move the primary booking panel higher on the page, and standardize currency display ("$1,499 MXN" everywhere).
